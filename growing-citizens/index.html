ROOTED IN RESEARCH

Strong learning systems make space for more than right answers.

This programme draws on practices used in Singapore, Finland, Japan, the United Kingdom and the United States—adapted for Indian families and the realities of a busy weekend.

It complements, rather than replaces, school. The aim is a capable child who can reason, communicate, create, care and contribute.
