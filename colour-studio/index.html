Beyond the Dictionary

60·30·10

Proportion

Equal amounts of every colour look flat. Give the softest about 60%, another about 30%, and keep the strongest for the 10% that becomes the focal point. The studio assigns roles this way; rotate them to test alternatives.

L·M·D

Value first

A strong set has a clear light, mid and dark. Use Value check to see the greys: if two colours turn the same grey, separate them, or lighten or darken one.

W/C

Temperature

Colours leaning towards orange come forward; colours leaning towards blue recede. Use that for depth on a canvas, in a room, or across an outfit.

Codes are a close guide, not a standard: Wada values come from Matt DesLauriers' open digitisation of the 2010 Seigensha reprint (MIT licence). Screens, inks, fabrics and pigments all differ, so always test a physical sample. Inspired by Sanzo Wada's A Dictionary of Color Combinations (Seigensha); not affiliated with the publisher.
