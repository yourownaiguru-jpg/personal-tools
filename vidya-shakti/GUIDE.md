# Guide: how to use Vidya Shakti

1. **Start with a goal** (optional). Tap *Start earning soon*, *Start a business*, *Move into tech*, *Finish my education* or *Return after a break*. Tap it again to clear.
2. **Tell it about you.** Choose your qualification and state. National schemes always show.
3. **Narrow down.** Pick an area of interest, a cost, or tick *Women-only*, *Career break* or *Open or rolling now*.
4. **Read a card.** The coloured tag shows status: *Open or rolling*, *Expected*, *Check site* or *Not announced*. Tap *See details* for eligibility and costs.
5. **Apply on the official site.** Use the *Official site* button. If none is listed, search the programme name with your district or state and confirm with the office.
6. **Dates to watch** (top right) lists upcoming deadlines; tap one to jump to it.

**Good to know:** nothing you choose is saved or sent anywhere. Dates marked *Expected* are guesses from past years.
